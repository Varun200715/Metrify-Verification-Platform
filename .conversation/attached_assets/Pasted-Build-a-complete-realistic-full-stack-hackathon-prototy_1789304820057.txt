Build a complete, realistic, full-stack hackathon prototype called:

METRIFY
Smart Digital Verification Platform for Measuring Instruments

============================================================
CORE CONCEPT
============================================================

Metrify is a single digital platform with TWO connected interfaces:

1. 🌐 METRIFY WEB PORTAL
   Used by:
   - Business Owners
   - Authorities/Admin
   - Citizens

2. 📱 METRIFY OFFICER APP
   Used by:
   - Legal Metrology Officers (LMOs)

Both interfaces must use the SAME backend and SAME PostgreSQL database.

The main workflow is:

Business registers instrument on Website
        ↓
Application enters Metrify system
        ↓
LMO receives inspection through Officer App
        ↓
LMO visits establishment
        ↓
AI OCR reads instrument details
        ↓
LMO performs physical inspection
        ↓
Inspection is saved
        ↓
If offline → store locally → sync later
        ↓
Verification record created
        ↓
Digital certificate generated
        ↓
Unique QR generated
        ↓
Citizen scans QR
        ↓
Citizen sees current verification status
        ↓
Inspection history / risk signals help authorities plan future inspections

IMPORTANT:
Metrify does NOT replace the physical inspection performed by an authorized Legal Metrology Officer.

AI only assists with data extraction and cross-checking.
The LMO remains responsible for the physical verification decision.
